## Deploy another stage

```sh
alchemy deploy --stage staging
```

Each stage gets its own template and Worker, named after the stage. Without `--stage`, you deploy to `live_$USER`.

## Share one E2B team

Stages share your E2B team. Sandboxes a stage's Worker creates carry the stage in their metadata, so destroying one stage kills only its own sandboxes:

```sh
alchemy destroy --stage staging
```

Volumes and templates you create at runtime have no metadata. Delete them when their owner goes away, as the example's `DELETE /users/:user` route does.
